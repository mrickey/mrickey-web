In addition to the features listed above there are some additional features on the roadmap that will be included with software updates.Cross band repeater function (analog-analog, analog-DMR mixture, DMR-DMR)

- Dual RX simultaneously (Analog+DMR or Analog +Analog)
- Allows UU , UV, VV and VU dual band
- Full duplex talk same as the mobile phone (with BT earpiece or wired earpiece)
- Two power transistor PA for higher power
- AES256 digital encryption
- PTT Override in digital channel (allows the supervisor to start the transmission while the worker is talking)
- Weather Alert
