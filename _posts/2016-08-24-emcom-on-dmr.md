<span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">Emergency Communications, or EMCOM, is a significant element of amateur radio. As new voice modes are added there is always interest in using the latest technology.</span><span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;"></span><span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">DMR on the Brandmeister network has taken a major step in making DMR much more usable for EMCOM.</span><span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;"></span><span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">The following announcement was posted in the BrandMeister Facebook group:</span>> <span style="color:blue;font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">Due to the high demand for emergency communications on DMR we have implemented two new (worldwide) talkgroups</span>- <span style="color:blue;font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">TG 9911 - EMCOM US</span>
> - <span style="color:blue;font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">TG 9112 - EMCOM EU</span>
> 
> <span style="color:blue;font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">Please keep these talkgroups clear. Use them only for Emergency Communications!</span><span style="color:blue;font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">(FAQ: why is there a 9 in front of each TG? The 9 stands for global TGs that are not bound to any country.)</span>

<span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;"></span><span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">For the [PAPA System](http://www.papasys.com/) we have decided to add EMCOM US (9911) to our code plugs. We intend to create an EMCOM zone with channels for each repeater. When needed, the PAPA System will make this talkgroup static on all of it's repeaters in the affected area.</span><span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;"></span><span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">This is being done to discourage use of the talkgroup, except during an emergency, and to make it easier to move between repeaters if you are using the talkgroup.</span><span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;"></span><span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;">Listen to the DMR Roundtable on 3106, Mondays at 8pm (Pacific Time) for updates.</span><span style="font-family:'georgia' , 'times new roman' , serif;"></span> *Special thanks to [qsl.net](http://www.qsl.net) for the image.*
