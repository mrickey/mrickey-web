<span style="font-family:'verdana' , sans-serif;">The BrandMeister Hose has been updated to include scanning capabilities. When you go to The Hose, you will now see a new "Scanner" option on the menu.</span><span style="font-family:'verdana' , sans-serif;">![](http://35.202.40.146/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/BM_Scanner1.png)</span><span style="font-family:'verdana' , sans-serif;">Clicking the "Scanner" button will show you a list of talkgroups. Put in the talkgroups you would like to listen to and click "Apply" </span>![](http://35.202.40.146/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/BM_Scanner2.png)<span style="font-family:'verdana' , sans-serif;">The scanner will start running and you will be able to listen to all of the talkgroups.</span><span style="font-family:'verdana' , sans-serif;"></span><span style="font-family:'verdana' , sans-serif;">Have fun!</span>
