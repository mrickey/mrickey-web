I long to be outdoors more. Not just a hike on the weekend, but really out there. Having the freedom to do what I want, when I want. Not having that has been difficult for me.

<figure class="wp-block-image alignright size-full is-resized">![](/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/2025-10-29-EzB.png)</figure>To get a feel for that lifestyle I live through the Youtube videos of many different people. One of them is [Eva zu Beck](https://www.youtube.com/@evazubeck). She is an inspiration to me, and has helped me to understand myself better.

After many years of travel she has written a book that I am very excited about. Her book, The Wilder Way, talks about how she got started along this path, the adventures she had along the way, her current life, and a little about where she's going.

I can't wait to read her book. It isn't getting released until June 2026, but I pre-ordered the kindle version already. There is also a hardcover.
